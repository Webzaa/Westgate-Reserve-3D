Privacy Policy

At House of Hiranandani, we are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors, customers and users of our websites and services. Personal information shared through an enquiry is treated responsibly and confidentially.

Information and its use

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, and communicate relevant updates about House of Hiranandani properties and services.

How your information is handled

House of Hiranandani does not authorize the selling or renting of your personal information to third parties for their business purposes. The privacy practices described here may be revised from time to time, and updated versions will be posted on the applicable website.

Communication

By submitting an enquiry and providing the required authorization, you consent to being contacted regarding your enquiry. Your phone number may be used to contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or other messaging services for relevant communication.

Questions about privacy

If you have questions about these privacy practices or would like more information, please contact House of Hiranandani through its official website.
